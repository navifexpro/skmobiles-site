Inside the SK Mobiles repairing lab

A small lab in the basement of Ashraf Plaza, Manga Mandi. Fixing phones since 2016.

Our story

Since 2016, one phone at a time.

SK Mobiles started in Manga Mandi in 2016. Since then we have repaired more than 1000 phones for people from Manga Mandi, Raiwind and all over Lahore. Some came with a cracked screen, some came dead, some needed work deep inside the board.

We do all the work ourselves. Our bench has a microscope, a soldering station, a DC power supply and board schematics. So when a phone needs chip level work, it stays right here with us.

MicroscopeFor tiny chips and joints
Soldering stationFor board and IC work
DC power supplyTo find power faults
Board schematicsTo trace the real problem
