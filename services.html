FAQ

Common questions

Is phone checking free?

Yes. Checking is free. We open and check your phone, tell you the fault, and give you the price. You decide after that.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the fault and the part. After checking, we tell you how much time your phone needs before we start any work.

Do you use original or copy parts?

Both are available. We tell you the price of each, and you choose the one you want.

Do you send phones outside for repair?

No. All board and chip work is done in our own lab in Manga Mandi. Your phone stays with us.

Which areas do you cover for Pick & Drop?

Pick & Drop is available in Lahore, including Manga Mandi, Raiwind, Bahria Town and Model Town. Message us on WhatsApp to set it up.

I live in another city. Can I send my phone by courier?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp first. We share the address, you send the phone by courier, we check it and tell you the price. After your OK we fix it and send it back.

How can I pay?

You can pay by cash, JazzCash, EasyPaisa or bank transfer.

Which brands do you repair?

All brands, including iPhone, Samsung, Vivo, Oppo, Infinix, Tecno, Xiaomi and Realme. We also repair tablets.

Tell us what is wrong.

Send your phone model and the problem on WhatsApp. We reply with the next step.
